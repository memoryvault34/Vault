Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open Vault → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it right away.

Does Vault sell my data or show ads?
No. Vault has no ads and never sells personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

My AI-generated caption or location is wrong — can I fix it?
Yes, every caption and detail in Vault can be edited or removed manually at any time.
